How tight or spread out your shots are landing. A tighter cluster means a more repeatable swing.
Attack Angle Distribution
The direction your club is moving at impact. Steeper swings hit down, shallower swings sweep through.
Smash Factor Trend
A measure of strike quality. The closer to 1.50, the more efficiently you are converting swing speed into ball speed.
Ball Speed Progression
How hard the ball left the face each shot. A drop toward the end of your session usually means fatigue setting in.
